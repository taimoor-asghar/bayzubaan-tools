    Paediatrics: two audiences, one conversation

    Paediatric German is the only clinical register with two audiences at once: the parents you address (Sie) and the child you talk about (Ihr Kind, Ihre Tochter, Ihr Sohn). The Fachsprachenprüfung tests whether you can keep both straight under pressure — Sie-form imperatives to the parents, third-person references to the child, and a tone that calms without minimising. This tool gives you 10 frames that cover the full parent conversation: the diagnosis, the reassurance, the treatment, the dosing instructions, the red flags, the kindergarten question, the history, and the closing.

    The opening pair is the whole art in miniature. Ihr Kind hat eine Mittelohrentzündung. — plain, jargon-free, named. Then the calibration: Das ist gut behandelbar. Four severity slots cover the territory: nicht gefährlich (not dangerous), gut behandelbar (easily treatable), harmlos (harmless), ernst, aber behandelbar (serious but treatable). Note the last one — when the news is genuinely serious, you say so and give the treatability in the same breath. Parents remember the calibration sentence long after they forget the diagnosis; examiners score it as empathy in German.

    Dosing instructions parents actually follow

    Parent dosing is where precision meets daily life. Geben Sie Ihrem Kind den Saft dreimal täglich. Geben Sie Ihrem Kind die Tropfen morgens und abends. Geben Sie Ihrem Kind das Zäpfchen bei Fieber über 38,5 Grad. Three details examiners listen for: the dative recipient (Ihrem Kind, Ihrer Tochter, Ihrem Sohn — get the case right, it is tested), the exact threshold (bei Fieber über 38,5 Grad — the number is the instruction), and the everyday reality (viel zu trinken — plenty to drink — belongs in every paediatric instruction set).

    The Zäpfchen (suppository) frame deserves attention: fever management in small children runs through suppositories, and parents need the threshold named explicitly — bei Fieber über 38,5 Grad, not "wenn es Fieber hat". The quiz tests exactly this one-slot precision: den Saft dreimal täglich vs. die Tropfen morgens und abends vs. das Zäpfchen bei Fieber über 38,5 Grad differ by one slot, and picking the wrong one is a real dosing error.

    Red flags: the sentence that protects the child

    Bei Trinkverweigerung kommen Sie bitte sofort wieder. The red-flag frame is the safety net of every paediatric consultation, and its four slots are the four deteriorations that matter: Fieber über 39 Grad (fever above 39), Atemnot (breathlessness), Trinkverweigerung (refusing to drink — the dehydration red flag parents underestimate), starke Schmerzen (severe pain). Trinkverweigerung is the word learners never knew and examiners always test: in small children, refusing fluids is the early warning sign, and naming it marks you as someone who thinks paediatrically.

    Note the structure: Bei {symptom} kommen Sie bitte sofort wieder — the symptom first (what to watch for), then the unambiguous instruction (sofort wieder — come back immediately, no hedging). A red flag with a hedged instruction is not a red flag.

    The kindergarten question and the history

    Every parent asks it, every examiner tests it: Ihr Kind darf wieder in den Kindergarten. The return-to-daily-life frame covers wieder in den Kindergarten, wieder zur Schule, wieder Sport machen, noch nicht schwimmen — and note the last one, the prohibition form (darf noch nicht), which tests negation placement in a modal construction. Parents plan their week around this sentence; get it fluent.

    The history frames are your German toolkit for the parent interview: Isst und trinkt Ihr Kind normal? (the intake question — always ask), Hat Ihr Kind Fieber / Durchfall / einen Ausschlag / Husten? (the symptom sweep). And the closing: Haben Sie Fragen zu der Dosierung? — always ask, and name the topic (der Behandlung, der Dosierung, dem Krankheitsverlauf), because parents' real questions surface only when invited.

    Reassurance without minimising

    The final frame is the tone of the whole conversation: Das wird wieder. Das geht vorbei. Machen Sie sich keine Sorgen. Three reassurance sentences, three different weights — use the first two for the routine, the third only when you mean it. The exam trap is reassurance that minimises: harmlos for something that is not harmless costs you the empathy marks. Calibrate honestly, reassure specifically, and the parents — and the examiner — trust you.

    How to drill: the parent round

    Build a full parent script for one diagnosis: Ihr Kind hat eine Mittelohrentzündung. Das ist gut behandelbar. Wir geben Ihrem Kind Tropfen für die Ohren. Geben Sie Ihrem Kind die Tropfen morgens und abends. Bei Fieber über 39 Grad kommen Sie bitte sofort wieder. Ihr Kind darf wieder in den Kindergarten. Haben Sie Fragen zu der Dosierung? Das wird wieder. Copy it and deliver it aloud, warmly, at a pace worried parents can follow — then swap every slot and run it again. The frames stay; the families change.

    Then take the quiz: every distractor differs by exactly one slot — wrong child, wrong dose, wrong threshold, wrong permission — so it tests the one-slot precision paediatrics demands. When you score 12/12, run the script from memory for a second diagnosis: that is the parent conversation, rehearsed.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I address parents vs. the child in German?

    Address the parents in the Sie-form (Geben Sie Ihrem Kind…) and refer to the child in the third person (Ihr Kind, Ihre Tochter, Ihr Sohn). Keep both straight — examiners test this under pressure.

    How do I tell parents a diagnosis is not dangerous?

    Das ist gut behandelbar / nicht gefährlich / harmlos. When it is genuinely serious: ernst, aber behandelbar — name the seriousness and the treatability in the same breath.

    How do I give dosing instructions to parents in German?

    Geben Sie Ihrem Kind den Saft dreimal täglich. Get the dative right (Ihrem Kind, Ihrer Tochter, Ihrem Sohn), name exact thresholds (bei Fieber über 38,5 Grad), and always add viel zu trinken.

    What is Trinkverweigerung and why does it matter?

    Refusing to drink — the dehydration red flag in small children that parents underestimate. Bei Trinkverweigerung kommen Sie bitte sofort wieder is the safety-net sentence examiners expect.

    How do I answer when the child can go back to kindergarten?

    Ihr Kind darf wieder in den Kindergarten / wieder zur Schule / wieder Sport machen — or the prohibition form: darf noch nicht schwimmen. Parents plan their week around this sentence.

    How do I reassure worried parents without minimising?

    Das wird wieder. Das geht vorbei. Machen Sie sich keine Sorgen — but calibrate honestly: calling something harmlos when it is not costs the empathy marks. Reassure specifically, not generically.
