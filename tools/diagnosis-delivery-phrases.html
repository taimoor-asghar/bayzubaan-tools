Bad news is a procedure — with steps

Delivering a diagnosis in German is not a vocabulary problem; it is a procedure problem. Like any procedure it has an indication, a preparation, a technique, and an aftercare. The international framework is SPIKES — Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Emotions, Strategy — and the German version of each step has fixed phrasing that examiners in the Fachsprachenprüfung listen for. This tool gives you 24 blunt-to-gentle rewrite pairs across the four phases: opening and preparation, serious diagnoses, prognosis, and sensitive findings. Each card shows what you must never say next to what a German doctor says instead, with the grammatical principle that makes the gentle version work.

The core insight: gentleness in German clinical speech is grammatical, not decorative. It lives in leider placed before the noun, in the Konjunktiv II (Ich wünschte, ich hätte bessere Nachrichten), in impersonal constructions (Es handelt sich um…), in evidential framing (Die Befunde sprechen für…), and in questions that hand control to the patient (Möchten Sie, dass jemand dabei ist?). None of these is "being nice". Each is a technique with a function: pacing, distancing, softening, or empowering. Learn the techniques and you can gentle-ify any diagnosis, including ones you have never delivered before.

Phase 1: preparation before knowledge

The six opening pairs teach the most violated rule of bad-news delivery: never start with the diagnosis. Möchten Sie, dass jemand bei diesem Gespräch dabei ist? comes before the news, not after — support must be in the room when the blow lands. Was wissen Sie bisher über Ihre Erkrankung? calibrates the starting point: telling a patient who already suspects cancer differs completely from telling one who expects reassurance, and the FSP examiners specifically watch for this calibration step.

Notice the pacing phrases: Nehmen Sie sich einen Moment Zeit — das ist viel auf einmal. Non-native candidates rush — silence after bad news feels like failure, so they fill it with more words. German clinical culture expects the pause; naming it (das ist viel auf einmal) turns an awkward silence into care. And the Konjunktiv II opener Ich wünschte, ich hätte bessere Nachrichten für Sie is the single most useful sentence in this whole tool: it signals bad news, expresses the doctor's humanity, and buys you three seconds to organise the next sentence — all in one grammatical form.

Phase 2: the diagnosis itself

The six diagnosis pairs show four softening techniques. Evidential framing: Die Untersuchungen zeigen, dass… / Die Befunde sprechen für… — attributing the diagnosis to the findings distances the doctor from the blow and sounds less absolute, which matters when the diagnosis is clinical rather than certain. Impersonal construction: Es handelt sich leider um einen bösartigen Tumor — the es handelt sich um frame is formal diagnostic German; it carries the news in institutional language, which paradoxically feels less personal and therefore less brutal.

Function-not-failure: Ihre Nierenfunktion ist leider stark eingeschränkt instead of Ihre Nieren versagen — name the limited function, not the failing organ; eingeschränkt (limited) is a state, versagen (to fail) is a verdict. Diagnosis-plus-manageability: Sie haben Diabetes — eine Erkrankung, mit der man bei guter Einstellung sehr gut leben kann. Every chronic diagnosis should be delivered with its manageability in the same breath; the dash is doing clinical work, bridging the blow and the hope before despair can settle in between.

Phase 3: prognosis without false certainty

The prognosis pairs teach the hardest discipline: honest uncertainty. Über den weiteren Verlauf kann ich Ihnen leider keine sichere Vorhersage machen is not evasion — it is the truth, and German patients respect it more than false precision. Time estimates are given as ranges with explicit uncertainty, never as dates; von Mensch zu Mensch sehr unterschiedlich says what every honest oncologist knows. Candidates who invent timelines to sound confident are marked down for it — in the exam and in real practice.

The palliative pivot deserves study: Auch wenn eine Heilung nicht möglich ist, gibt es vieles, was wir für Sie tun können. The auch wenn (even if) concession structure acknowledges the loss and pivots to care in one sentence. Wir werden alles tun, um Ihre Beschwerden so gut wie möglich zu lindern answers the patient's unspoken fear — abandonment — directly: there is always something to do, and symptom relief is treatment too. And Es ist sinnvoll, dass wir auch über Ihre Wünsche für die kommende Zeit sprechen introduces advance-care planning as sensible rather than as surrender — the framing determines whether the patient hears planning or giving up.

Phase 4: the sensitive findings

The last six pairs handle diagnoses that carry stigma, grief, or shame: HIV, disability, infertility, dementia, suicide attempt, alcohol misuse. The techniques intensify here. For HIV: extra framing time and an explicit next step in the same sentence (lassen Sie uns in Ruhe besprechen, was das bedeutet). For the child's impairment: person-first, future-oriented, support-naming — Ihr Kind wird mit einer Beeinträchtigung leben — wir besprechen, welche Unterstützung es gibt — never a label dropped alone. For infertility: acknowledge the loss, then open alternatives (es gibt jedoch andere Wege) without rushing past the grief.

The last two pairs teach question-as-technique. Können Sie mir erzählen, was in Ihnen vorgegangen ist? — vorgegangen (going on inside) avoids labelling the act; the open invitation is the opposite of an accusation, and in the FSP's psychiatry-adjacent scenarios this phrasing is exactly what is scored. Machen Sie sich Sorgen um Ihren Alkoholkonsum? asks about their worry, not their behaviour — patients who would lie to Sie trinken zu viel will answer honestly about their own concern. The question does the diagnosis; the doctor just holds the space.

How to drill: the rewrite reflex

Work through the 24 cards blunt-first: read the red sentence, look away, and produce the gentle version from memory before revealing it. The goal is not memorising 24 sentences — it is building the rewrite reflex, so that when your brain produces Sie haben Krebs under exam stress, your mouth automatically reaches for Ich muss Ihnen leider… instead. Do six cards a day; the reflex builds in about a week.

Then take the quiz: it tests situation-to-phrasing with distractors that are all real gentle phrases from other pairs — so the answer is never "the polite one" but "the right one for this exact moment". The HIV answer differs from the cancer answer differs from the dementia answer, and knowing why is the clinical judgment the FSP rewards. When you score 12/12, practise aloud: deliver one diagnosis from each category to an imaginary patient, with the pauses. That is the exam's hardest five minutes, rehearsed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SPIKES framework for delivering bad news?

SPIKES stands for Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Emotions, Strategy/Summary — the international standard for breaking bad news. The German phrasing for each step (Möchten Sie, dass jemand dabei ist? / Was wissen Sie bisher?) is what this tool teaches.

Why is leider so important in German bad-news delivery?

Leider (unfortunately) placed before the diagnosis signals the news before the noun lands, softening the blow. Ich muss Ihnen leider eine ernsthafte Diagnose mitteilen prepares the patient in a way the same sentence without leider does not.

How do I give a prognosis without lying or crushing hope?

State honest uncertainty: Über den weiteren Verlauf kann ich Ihnen leider keine sichere Vorhersage machen. Give ranges, never dates, and always pair the limitation with what can still be done.

What is the difference between sprechen für and zeigen?

Die Befunde sprechen für (the findings point to) leaves diagnostic room and sounds less absolute; Die Untersuchungen zeigen, dass (the examinations show that) states a confirmed finding. Use sprechen für for clinical diagnoses, zeigen for confirmed ones.

How do I ask about a possible suicide attempt?

With an open, non-judgemental invitation: Können Sie mir erzählen, was in Ihnen vorgegangen ist? — vorgegangen (going on inside) avoids labelling the act, and the question opens the door instead of making an accusation.

Is delivering bad news tested in the Fachsprachenprüfung?

Yes — the conversation part includes delivering findings and diagnoses, and examiners score patient-centred phrasing: preparation, calibration, gentle delivery, emotional space, and a clear next step. Blunt delivery loses marks even when the medicine is right.
