Why lab values are Fachsprachenprüfung gold

Few things separate a convincing Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch from a shaky one like lab values. The examiners hand you blood results and expect you to present them — Das Kreatinin liegt bei 1,4 mg/dl, leicht erhöht; die GFR beträgt 55 ml/min — with the right German term, the right article, and a clinical interpretation in the same breath. Textbook German teaches you der Arzt; the exam tests whether you can say die Thrombozyten liegen bei 220.000 pro Mikroliter without hesitating. This tool gives you the 40 values that appear most often, organised the way a Befund is organised, each with a real reference-range sentence you can steal for your presentations.

The six groups mirror the panels on a German lab slip: Blutbild (blood count), Leberwerte (liver), Nierenwerte (kidney), Elektrolyte (electrolytes), Stoffwechsel (metabolic), and Gerinnung (clotting). Learn them group by group — your brain stores words best in the context where they occur, and a German lab report is exactly the context where these words live. Every card carries the article: drill them as das-Kreatinin single units, never the bare noun.

Das Blutbild: the opening of every lab presentation

The blood count opens almost every case presentation, so these seven are your bread and butter: das Hämoglobin (Hb), der Hämatokrit, die roten Blutkörperchen (Erythrozyten), die weißen Blutkörperchen (Leukozyten), die Thrombozyten, das MCV, and die Blutsenkung (BSG). Learn the three reporting verbs: liegt bei (is at), beträgt (amounts to), and ist erhöht / erniedrigt (is raised / lowered). Das Hämoglobin liegt bei 14,2 g/dl — im Referenzbereich. Three clauses, and you have reported one value completely: the number, the unit, the judgment. Repeat that skeleton for every value and the examiner hears a doctor, not a student.

Note the clinical reading each example carries, because examiners reward interpretation, not recitation: elevated Leukozyten → infection (Hinweis auf eine Infektion); low red cells → anaemia (Verdacht auf Blutarmut); raised MCV → macrocytosis (die roten Blutkörperchen sind zu groß). The pattern X ist erhöht — Hinweis auf Y is the single most useful sentence template in the whole tool.

Leber- und Nierenwerte: the organ panels

Liver values: das Bilirubin, das ALT (GPT), das AST (GOT), die Gamma-GT, die alkalische Phosphatase (AP), das Albumin. Two notes for the exam. First, German labs use both names — ALT and GPT are the same enzyme; say both the first time (das ALT (GPT)) so nobody doubts you. Second, the Gamma-GT reading: Hinweis auf Gallenstau oder Alkohol — this is the value examiners probe when a case smells of liver, and the one you must interpret, not just name. Low Albumin means synthetic failure (Hinweis auf Leberschwäche), the liver panel's real red flag.

Kidney values: das Kreatinin, der Harnstoff, die GFR, das Cystatin C. The modern presentation leads with the GFR, not the creatinine: Die GFR beträgt 55 ml/min — Stadium 3 der Niereninsuffizienz. Knowing the staging sentence turns a number into a diagnosis. Das Cystatin C ist ein empfindlicher Nierenmarker — drop this in an Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch and you sound like someone who reads current guidelines.

Elektrolyte: where precision is safety

Electrolytes are the values where wrong German becomes dangerous German, so learn them cold: das Natrium, das Kalium, das Kalzium, das Magnesium, das Phosphat. The critical sentence is the potassium one: Das Kalium ist mit 5,8 mmol/l zu hoch — EKG-Kontrolle! Note the construction ist mit X zu hoch — value first, judgment second, action third. That is the rhythm of ward German: number, verdict, consequence. Magnesium wird bei Herzrhythmusstörungen substituiert — substituieren is the ward verb for replacing an electrolyte; geben works, but substituieren marks you as an insider.

Stoffwechsel und Entzündung: the metabolic story

The metabolic group is the longest because it is the most conversational — these are the values you explain to patients, not just colleagues: der Blutzucker, der HbA1c, das Cholesterin, das LDL-Cholesterin, das HDL-Cholesterin, die Triglyceride, das CRP, das TSH, das Vitamin D, das Eisen, das Ferritin, die Harnsäure, der PSA-Wert. Two patient-explanation patterns to master. First, the good/bad framing: HDL ist das „gute“ Cholesterin — je höher, desto besser. The je … desto construction is pure B2 grammar doing real clinical work. Second, the consequence sentence: Die Triglyceride sind erhöht — weniger Alkohol und Zucker. Value, verdict, advice — the patient hears what to do, not just what is wrong.

Three interpretation pairs examiners love: Blutzucker and HbA1c (moment vs. three-month average — der Zucker war zu hoch eingestellt), Eisen and Ferritin (level vs. stores — bitte Ferritin mitbestimmen), TSH direction (low TSH → Verdacht auf Überfunktion — the inverse catches everyone the first time). And das CRP ist mit 45 mg/l deutlich erhöht — deutlich (clearly) calibrates the severity in one adverb; leicht and deutlich are the two adverbs that do most of the interpretive work in German lab presentations.

Gerinnung: the clotting five

Der INR-Wert, der Quick-Wert, die aPTT, das D-Dimer, das Fibrinogen. The INR sentence every anticoagulated patient needs: Der INR liegt bei 2,5 — im therapeutischen Bereich. Im therapeutischen Bereich (in the therapeutic range) is the phrase that closes the question "is it right?" — learn it as a unit. The D-Dimer logic is the classic rule-out: Das D-Dimer ist negativ — Thrombose unwahrscheinlich. And die aPTT ist unter Heparin verlängert — note unter with medication: unter Heparin, unter Marcumar. That preposition is small, fixed, and examined.

How to drill: from cards to case presentation

Browse each group and read the example sentences aloud — they are built from the three reporting skeletons (liegt bei / beträgt / ist erhöht–erniedrigt) plus a judgment clause, and every one is stealable. When you can chain five values into one flowing paragraph, the lab section of your Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch is done.

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a lab value in German?

Use one of three skeletons: Das Kreatinin liegt bei 1,4 mg/dl / Die GFR beträgt 55 ml/min / Das Kalium ist erhöht. Then add the judgment: im Referenzbereich, leicht erhöht, deutlich erhöht, erniedrigt.

What is the difference between ALT and GPT?

None — they are the same enzyme. German labs use both names; say das ALT (GPT) the first time so nobody doubts you. Same for AST (GOT).

How do I say a value is in the normal range?

Im Referenzbereich or in der Norm. For deviations, calibrate with leicht (slightly) or deutlich (clearly): Das CRP ist deutlich erhöht.

Which lab interpretation do examiners probe most?

The low-TSH trap (low TSH means suspected hyperthyroidism, not hypo-), the HbA1c vs. Blutzucker distinction, and reading the GFR as a kidney-failure stage.

What does 'im therapeutischen Bereich' mean?

In the therapeutic range — the standard verdict for an INR under anticoagulation: Der INR liegt bei 2,5 — im therapeutischen Bereich.

How do Germans say 'under heparin'?

Unter Heparin — the fixed preposition for being on a medication: Die aPTT ist unter Heparin verlängert. Same with unter Marcumar.
