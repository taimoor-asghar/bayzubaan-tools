What B2 means (and what this test checks)

B2 (Upper-intermediate) is fluent independent use: you interact with native speakers without strain, produce clear detailed text, and argue with pros and cons. It's the level most professional contexts require. The grammar leap from B1 is about register and precision: Konjunktiv I for reported speech (er sagte, er sei krank) — the hallmark of formal German, from news to academia; sein-passive vs werden-passive (die Tür ist geöffnet = state, wird geöffnet = process); nominalizations (das Lesen, die Voraussetzung) that power formal style; participles as adjectives (die geschlossene Tür); extended attributes (der in Berlin lebende Mann) that compress whole clauses into noun phrases; double infinitives (hat machen können); future perfect; correlative conjunctions (nicht nur … sondern auch, sowohl … als auch); and a deep bench of prepositional verbs (abhängen von, bestehen auf, gespannt sein auf). Vocabulary crosses 4,000 words, with collocations (in Frage stellen, sich aneignen) and register control. This test's 25 questions sample all of it — each tagged with its topic, so your result is a diagnosis, not just a score.

Common B2 traps

B2's traps punish B1 habits. Konjunktiv I vs II: reported speech takes Konjunktiv I (er sagte, er sei) — Konjunktiv II (wäre) signals doubt or unreality, not neutral reporting. ist geöffnet vs wird geöffnet: state vs process — die Tür ist geöffnet (it stands open) vs wird geöffnet (someone is opening it). Extended attributes: the participle phrase takes the adjective ending of its position — der in Berlin lebende Mann (nominative masculine, weak: -e). weshalb vs deshalb: weshalb opens a relative clause (der Grund, weshalb…), deshalb starts a main clause. Prepositional verbs: the preposition is arbitrary and must be memorised with the verb — bestehen auf (+ dative), gespannt sein auf (+ accusative). B2 to C1: the bridge

C1 is effective operational proficiency: academic and professional German — nuanced argumentation, implicit meaning, controlled register shifts. The bridge from B2: full Nominalstil (noun-heavy academic style), Konjunktiv I in all tenses including future, participle constructions replacing clauses, functional verb phrases (Funktionsverbgefüge: zur Anwendung kommen, in Betracht ziehen), concessive and modal particles (durchaus, immerhin, ja, eben) used with native precision, and vocabulary past 8,000 words with near-native collocation control. What does B2 German level mean?

What is the difference between 'ist geöffnet' and 'wird geöffnet'?

ist geöffnet is the sein-passive = state (the door stands open); wird geöffnet is the werden-passive = process (the door is being opened).

When do I use Konjunktiv I vs Konjunktiv II?

Konjunktiv I for neutral reported speech (er sagte, er sei krank). Konjunktiv II for doubt, hypotheticals, and wishes (wenn ich reich wäre).

What are extended attributes in German?

Participle phrases placed before a noun, declined like adjectives: der in Berlin lebende Mann (the man living in Berlin). A hallmark of written B2+ German.

What comes after passing B2?
