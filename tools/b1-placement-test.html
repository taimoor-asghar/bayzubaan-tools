What B1 means (and what this test checks)

B1 (Intermediate) is the independence threshold — the level employers, universities, and visa offices ask for. At B1 you can handle travel situations, describe experiences and ambitions, and give reasons for opinions — in connected, if simple, discourse. The grammar leap from A2 is substantial: full subordinate clause repertoire (obwohl, damit, falls, ohne dass), Konjunktiv II past (hätte gemacht), passive in all tenses (wurde gebaut, ist gebaut worden), n-declension (den Studenten, dem Kollegen), relative clauses with prepositions (das Haus, in dem ich wohne), da-/wo-compounds (darauf, worüber), infinitive constructions (um zu, ohne zu, anstatt zu), genitive prepositions (wegen, trotz), and Präteritum of modals and irregulars (konnte, wusste). Vocabulary crosses 2,000 words. This test's 25 questions sample all of it — each tagged with its topic, so your result is a diagnosis, not just a score.

The 25 questions, decoded

1–2 open with subordinate conjunctions: concessive obwohl vs causal weil, and purpose um … zu vs damit (infinitive subject → um zu; different subject → damit). 3 is Konjunktiv II past — the hätte gehabt double infinitive that defines B1 hypotheticals. 4–5 test passive across tenses: wurde gebaut (Präteritum) vs worden (the participle that trips everyone — not *geworden in the passive!). 6–7 probe n-declension, the masculine -n endings (den Studenten, dem Kollegen) that A2 never required. 8–9 are relative clauses with prepositions — case comes from the preposition's question (wo? → in dem; wohin? → in die). 10–11 check als vs wie, the comparison pair learners mix for years. 12–13 test da-/wo-compounds (darauf, worüber) — the pronominal adverbs of fluent speech. 14 is ohne zu. 15–16, 25 sample B1 vocabulary (Bewerbung, zuverlässig, sich ereignen). 17–18 Präteritum of modals and irregulars. 19–20 adjective endings in weak and mixed declension. 21–22 verb-final word order in subclauses. 23–24 genitive prepositions. Common B1 traps

B1's traps are subtler than A2's. Worden vs geworden: passive perfect takes worden (ist gebaut worden); geworden is for werden as a main verb (er ist Arzt geworden). Um zu vs damit: same subject → um zu (ich lerne, um zu arbeiten); different subject → damit (ich lerne, damit du stolz bist). n-declension: masculine nouns like Student, Kollege, Name, Herr take -n/-en in every case but nominative — den Namen, dem Herrn. Als vs wie: inequality → als (größer als), equality → wie (so groß wie); *als wie is the classic error. da-compounds: preposition + da(r) replaces es + preposition for things (darauf, not *auf es) — but never for people. Master these five and B1 grammar loses its fear factor.

B1 to B2: the bridge

B2 is upper-intermediate: fluent interaction, clear detailed text, argumentation with pros and cons. The bridge from B1: Konjunktiv I (indirect speech — er sagte, er sei krank), nominalizations (Nominalstil), participles as adjectives (die gerade eingetroffene E-Mail), extended adjective constructions, all passive forms including sein-passive (die Tür ist geöffnet vs wird geöffnet), and vocabulary past 4,000 words with collocations and register control. Frequently asked questions

What does B1 German level mean?

B1 (Intermediate): independent everyday use — travel, experiences, opinions with reasons. Grammar: full subordinate clauses, Konjunktiv II past, passive tenses, n-declension, relative clauses with prepositions, da-/wo-compounds, infinitive constructions, genitive prepositions. Vocabulary 2000+ words.

How is this B1 placement test scored?

25 questions: 0–8 below B1, 9–14 B1.1, 15–19 B1.2, 20–22 B1 pass (ready for B2), 23–25 above B1. Your result includes a study plan with links to the exact tools for your weak topics.

What is the difference between 'worden' and 'geworden'?

Passive perfect uses worden (ist gebaut worden); geworden is the participle of werden as a main verb (er ist Arzt geworden). Mixing them is the classic B1 error.

When do I use 'um zu' vs 'damit'?

Same subject → um zu (ich lerne, um zu arbeiten). Different subject → damit (ich lerne, damit du stolz bist).

What is the n-declension?

Masculine nouns like Student, Kollege, Name, Herr take -n/-en in all cases except nominative: den Studenten, dem Kollegen, des Namens. A core B1 topic.

What comes after passing B1?

B2: Konjunktiv I, nominalizations, participles as adjectives, sein-passive, 4000+ words with collocations. Take the B2 placement test the week you pass B1.
