    The Entlassungsbrief: the document that never leaves the patient

    The Entlassungsbrief — the discharge summary — is the single most-read document in German medicine. The GP reads it, the patient reads it, the next hospital reads it, and in the Fachsprachenprüfung the examiners make you dictate one: the Dokumentation station is, in practice, an Entlassungsbrief spoken aloud. It follows a fixed architecture — Diagnose, Aufnahme, Anamnese, Befund, Diagnostik, Therapie, Verlauf, Medikation, Procedere, Arbeitsunfähigkeit — and the candidate who walks that architecture without prompting sounds like a German Assistenzarzt. This tool gives you 10 frames, one per section, with swappable slots and a 12-round quiz on the exact sentences.

    The style rule that governs everything: German medical documentation is impersonal. No ich, no wir — the therapy happens, the course was, the finding shows. Therapeutisch erfolgte eine antibiotische Therapie. Der Verlauf war komplikationslos. This nominal, passive style is not optional colour; examiners mark the candidate who dictates "I gave antibiotics" as not yet thinking in German medical register. Every frame in this tool is built in that style — steal the skeletons.

    Diagnose and Aufnahme: the header of the story

    Entlassungsdiagnose: akute Appendizitis. The discharge diagnosis opens the letter, and in German it takes the colon form — diagnosis first, explanation later. The four slot options cover the classic discharge cases: akute Appendizitis, Pneumonie rechts, Herzinsuffizienz NYHA II, Diabetes mellitus Typ 2, entgleist. Note entgleist (decompensated) — the single word that turns a chronic diagnosis into an admission reason.

    Die Aufnahme erfolgte als Notfall über die Notaufnahme. The admission sentence establishes the story's beginning: emergency vs. elective changes everything that follows. Erfolgte — the past of erfolgen — is the documentation verb par excellence: Die Aufnahme erfolgte …, therapeutisch erfolgte …, die Einstellung erfolgte … Learn it as the default verb of the Entlassungsbrief and half your sentences write themselves.

    Anamnese, Befund, Diagnostik: the evidence chain

    Anamnestisch bestehen seit drei Tagen Bauchschmerzen. The history frame uses the impersonal bestehen with the symptom as subject — the classic German construction that avoids "the patient has". Alternatives: berichtet der Patient über … (the patient reports — for subjective symptoms), ist … bekannt (is known — for past history), klagt der Patient über … (complains of — slightly stronger). Four verbs, four registers of history-taking — the examiner hears which one you choose.

    Klinisch Druckschmerz im rechten Unterbauch. The examination frame is telegraphic: no verb at all, just the finding. Klinisch + noun phrase is the complete German examination note, and the quiz tests exactly this compression — the candidate who adds a verb ("Klinisch gibt es Druckschmerz") has not yet learned the register. Diagnostisch bestätigte das Röntgen die Pneumonie. The workup frame restores the verb but keeps it factual: zeigten sich (were seen), bestätigte (confirmed), ergab (yielded), war erhöht (was elevated).

    Therapie and Verlauf: what was done, how it went

    Therapeutisch wurde eine laparoskopische Appendektomie durchgeführt. The therapy frame is passive by design — wurde … durchgeführt (was performed), erfolgte (was given), wurde … optimiert (was optimised). The doer is irrelevant; the deed is the documentation. Then the Verlauf — the one sentence examiners always ask for: Der Verlauf war komplikationslos. Four options cover the reality: komplikationslos (the word every doctor wants to write), erfreulich mit rascher Besserung (favourable), verzögert bei Wundinfektion (delayed — with the reason attached, as German documentation demands), stabil unter Therapie (stable).

    Medikation, Procedere, AU: the handover

    The last three frames are the handover — what the GP actually acts on. Entlassungsmedikation: Metoprolol 50 mg 1-0-0. The 1-0-0 notation (morning–midday–evening) is universal in German prescribing; writing it correctly is a small signal of system fluency. Procedere: Wiedervorstellung beim Hausarzt in einer Woche. The Procedere is the plan in imperative-noun form: Wiedervorstellung (review), Fadenzug (suture removal), Kontrolle der Nierenwerte (recheck). Each names who does what by when — the three coordinates of a safe handover.

    Arbeitsunfähigkeit besteht für 2 Wochen. The sick-note sentence closes the letter. Besteht (exists/is certified) vs. ist nicht erforderlich (not required) — the binary that decides the patient's next weeks. Note the impersonal subject: the Arbeitsunfähigkeit exists; nobody "gives" it in German documentation.

    Abbreviations and dictation traps

    German discharge letters are dense with abbreviations, and the FSP tests whether you expand them correctly when dictating: Z. n. (Zustand nach — status post), V. a. (Verdacht auf — suspected), bds. (beidseits — bilateral), o. p. B. (ohne pathologischen Befund — no pathological findings), k. A. (kein Anhalt — no evidence). Dictate them expanded the first time — Zustand nach laparoskopischer Appendektomie — because the examiner scores the spoken form, not the written shortcut. Three dictation traps catch candidates every session: saying der instead of die before feminine Latin nouns (die Pneumonie, die Appendizitis), dropping the colon after Entlassungsdiagnose (the colon is the structure), and forgetting the unit on every number (1,4 mg/dl, never bare 1,4). Drill the letter above until the abbreviations expand automatically and the articles land without thought.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the standard sections of a German Entlassungsbrief?

    Diagnose, Aufnahme, Anamnese, Befund, Diagnostik, Therapie, Verlauf, Medikation, Procedere, Arbeitsunfähigkeit — walk them in order and you sound like a German Assistenzarzt.

    Why is German medical documentation impersonal?

    No ich, no wir — the therapy happens, the course was, the finding shows. Therapeutisch erfolgte eine antibiotische Therapie. Examiners mark candidates who dictate 'I gave antibiotics' as not yet in the register.

    How do I write 'the course was uncomplicated'?

    Der Verlauf war komplikationslos. For other courses: erfreulich mit rascher Besserung, verzögert bei Wundinfektion, stabil unter Therapie — always with the reason attached.

    How is discharge medication written in German?

    Entlassungsmedikation: Metoprolol 50 mg 1-0-0 — the 1-0-0 notation means morning–midday–evening and is universal in German prescribing.

    What belongs in the Procedere?

    Who does what by when: Wiedervorstellung beim Hausarzt in einer Woche, Fadenzug in 10 Tagen, Kontrolle der Nierenwerte in 2 Wochen. The three coordinates of a safe handover.

    How is Arbeitsunfähigkeit documented?

    Arbeitsunfähigkeit besteht für 2 Wochen — or ist nicht erforderlich. The Arbeitsunfähigkeit exists impersonally; nobody 'gives' it in German documentation.
