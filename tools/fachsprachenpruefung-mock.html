The Fachsprachenprüfung: three stations, one language

The Fachsprachenprüfung (FSP) — the medical German exam every foreign doctor must pass to practise in Germany — runs as three stations of 20 minutes each: the Arzt-Patienten-Gespräch (doctor-patient conversation: history-taking and explanation), the Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch (doctor-doctor conversation: structured case presentation), and the Dokumentation (documentation: dictating the Arztbrief or writing the referral). Same medicine, three different registers — and the exam fails candidates who bring the wrong register to the wrong station. This tool is a complete mock: six scenarios (two per station) with case vignettes, task checklists, and key phrases, a 20-minute countdown timer, and a 12-round station-tagged quiz on the exact sentences examiners listen for.

The stations test different German. Station 1 is spoken, simple, and patient-facing: short sentences, no jargon, constant checking of understanding. Station 2 is collegial and dense: structured presentation, lab values, therapy proposals, Konjunktiv-free directness. Station 3 is written register spoken aloud: impersonal, nominal, abbreviated. The candidate who code-switches between all three — Was führt Sie zu uns? to the patient, Ich halte eine Dialyseeinleitung für indiziert to the colleague, Der Verlauf war komplikationslos to the dictaphone — is the candidate who passes.

Station 1: Arzt-Patienten-Gespräch — the human station

Two scenarios. First, Herr Weber's chest pain: the classic emergency anamnesis. The tasks walk the station's real choreography — greeting and introduction, pain history (localisation, radiation, duration), risk factors (smoking, blood pressure, family), then explaining the ECG and blood draw. The key phrases are the station's greatest hits: Was führt Sie zu uns? (the standard opening — never "Was haben Sie?"), Wo genau tut es weh?, Strahlt der Schmerz aus?, Wir machen jetzt ein EKG und nehmen Blut ab. Note the Sie-form throughout and the explanation before the procedure — the exam scores patient-centredness as well as grammar.

Second, Frau Kaya's diabetes: the chronic-care conversation. The tasks — current wellbeing, previous medication, demonstrating the insulin pen, hypoglycaemia teaching — test the explaining register: Ich zeige Ihnen den Insulinpen. Bei Unterzucker: Traubenzucker nehmen. Short, imperative, checkable. The FSP examiners play the patient and will ask "Was heißt das?" — the candidate who can rephrase in simpler German (Der Zucker im Blut wird zu niedrig) passes the communication test that fails so many technically strong doctors.

Station 2: Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch — the presentation station

Two scenarios. First, the pneumonia case presentation: the tasks enforce the structure examiners expect — age and history, examination, labs, therapy proposal, plan. The key phrases are presentation skeletons: Ich stelle Ihnen Herrn Schneider vor. Laborchemisch zeigt sich ein erhöhtes CRP. Ich schlage eine antibiotische Therapie vor. Procedere: Verlaufskontrolle in 48 Stunden. Note laborchemisch — the single adverb that opens every lab presentation — and ich schlage … vor, the collegial proposal form (not "we should", not "I will").

Second, the kidney-failure consultation: the high-stakes numbers conversation. Das Kreatinin liegt bei 3,2 mg/dl. Die GFR beträgt 18 ml/min. Then the justification sentence the whole station turns on: Ich halte eine Dialyseeinleitung für indiziert. Für indiziert halten is the formal medical opinion construction — stronger than "I think", standard in consultations. Close with Bitte um nephrologische Mitbeurteilung. The candidate who presents numbers, interprets them, and states the consequence in three sentences has mastered station 2.

Station 3: Dokumentation — the written register

Two scenarios. First, dictating the appendectomy discharge letter: the tasks — diagnosis, course, medication, plan plus sick note — are the Entlassungsbrief in miniature. The key phrases are pure documentation German: Entlassungsdiagnose: akute Appendizitis. Der Verlauf war komplikationslos. Entlassungsmedikation: Ibuprofen 600 mg bei Bedarf. Arbeitsunfähigkeit besteht für 2 Wochen. Impersonal, colon-structured, abbreviated — the register of tools 78 and 79, now under time pressure.

Second, writing the cardiology referral: Ich überweise Ihnen meinen Patienten zur Mitbeurteilung. Verdachtsdiagnose: koronare Herzkrankheit. Fragestellung: Ausschluss einer KHK. Um zeitnahe Vorstellung wird gebeten. The four referral pillars in four sentences. The exam's documentation station often pairs exactly these two tasks — one Arztbrief, one Überweisung — because together they cover the complete written output of a German doctor's day.

The timer: training under pressure

The 20-minute timer is not decoration — it is the exam's defining constraint. Real FSP stations end at 20:00 whether you are finished or not, and candidates who have never practised against the clock discover too late that their beautiful German takes 35 minutes. Use the timer the way athletes use intervals: run one scenario at 20 minutes, check off the tasks as you complete them, and note where the time went. The 10- and 5-minute presets are for drilling single scenarios or the quiz under pressure. When you can complete all four tasks of a scenario with time to spare, the station is yours.

How to drill: the full mock

Run the exam end to end: station 1 (pick a scenario, start the timer, speak the anamnesis aloud, check the tasks), station 2 (present the case to an imaginary colleague, numbers first), station 3 (dictate the letter or write the referral against the clock). Then take the 12-round quiz — 4 rounds per station, each tagged, each testing a sentence from the key-phrase lists with one-slot distractors. Score 12/12 twice, then run the whole mock again from memory: three stations, three registers, one passing candidate.

Frequently asked questions

What are the three FSP stations?

Arzt-Patienten-Gespräch (20 min: history-taking and explanation), Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch (20 min: structured case presentation), Dokumentation (20 min: dictating the Arztbrief or writing the referral). Same medicine, three different registers.

How should I open the patient conversation?

Was führt Sie zu uns? — the standard opening. Never 'Was haben Sie?' Keep it Sie-form, short sentences, no jargon, and explain before every procedure.

How do I structure the case presentation in station 2?

Ich stelle Ihnen Herrn X vor — age, history, examination, labs (laborchemisch zeigt sich …), therapy proposal (ich schlage … vor), plan (Procedere: …). Numbers first, interpretation second, consequence third.

What is the documentation register?

Impersonal, nominal, abbreviated: Entlassungsdiagnose: akute Appendizitis. Der Verlauf war komplikationslos. No ich, no wir — the register of the Entlassungsbrief and Überweisung tools.

How do I justify a therapy to a colleague in German?

Ich halte eine Dialyseeinleitung für indiziert — für indiziert halten is the formal medical opinion construction, stronger than 'I think' and standard in consultations.

Why practise with the 20-minute timer?

Real FSP stations end at 20:00 whether you are finished or not. Candidates who never practise against the clock discover too late that their German takes 35 minutes. Drill scenarios as intervals until all four tasks fit with time to spare.
