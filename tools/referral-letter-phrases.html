    Die Überweisung: the collegial letter

    The Überweisung — the referral letter — is the most collegial document in German medicine: one doctor writing to another, in the Sie-form, with a clear question. In the Fachsprachenprüfung it appears as the written task behind the Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch: after presenting the case you are expected to formulate the referral, and the examiners score whether your German carries the four pillars — Anlass (reason), Befunde (findings), Fragestellung (the question), and Dringlichkeit (urgency). This tool gives you 10 frames that build the complete letter, with swappable slots and a 12-round quiz on the exact sentences.

    The register is formal-collegial throughout: Ich überweise Ihnen meinen Patienten Herrn Müller (62 J.) zur kardiologischen Mitbeurteilung. Note the grammar examiners listen for — Ihnen (dative, formal), meinen Patienten Herrn Müller (accusative double marking), zur Mitbeurteilung (zu + der). One sentence, three case decisions, and the candidate who lands all three has passed the hidden grammar test of the referral.

    Anlass and Verdachtsdiagnose: why you are writing

    Ich überweise Ihnen meine Patientin Frau Schmidt (45 J.) zur neurologischen Abklärung. The opening frame names the patient and the purpose in one breath. The four purpose nouns are the complete referral vocabulary: Mitbeurteilung (co-assessment — you want an opinion alongside your care), Abklärung (workup — you want the unclear clarified), Weiterbehandlung (ongoing care — you are handing over), Zweitmeinung (second opinion — you want confirmation or correction). Choosing the wrong one misdirects the referral: Abklärung when you mean Weiterbehandlung sends the patient back to you instead of onward. Verdachtsdiagnose: koronare Herzkrankheit. The suspected diagnosis follows immediately — with Verdachtsdiagnose, never a bare diagnosis, because the referral is a question, not a verdict.

    Befunde, Therapie, Medikation: the evidence so far

    Bisherige Befunde: EKG unauffällig, Troponin negativ. The findings frame is telegraphic — the specialist needs facts, not prose. Bisherige Therapie: Physiotherapie ohne Besserung. Document what was tried and that it failed: ohne Besserung justifies the referral more than any adjective. Aktuelle Medikation: Ramipril 5 mg 1-0-0. The medication line uses the same 1-0-0 notation as the Entlassungsbrief — consistency across documents is itself a professional signal. Note keine Dauermedikation (no regular medication): stating the negative explicitly is better than leaving the line empty, because the specialist cannot tell an empty line from a forgotten one.

    Fragestellung: the heart of the letter

    Fragestellung: Ausschluss einer koronaren Herzkrankheit. The clinical question is the sentence the whole letter exists for — many German specialists read the Fragestellung first and the rest only if needed. Formulate it as precisely as the referral allows: Ausschluss einer … (exclusion of — the most common referral question in medicine), Bitte um MRT-Befundung und Therapieempfehlung (request for reporting and recommendation — two verbs, one sentence), Bitte um Biopsie und Histologie (request for the procedure and its result), Indikation zur operativen Therapie? (the question mark does the asking). A vague Fragestellung — Bitte um Mitbeurteilung alone — is the classic referral failure: the specialist cannot tell what you want, and the examiners mark exactly that.

    Dringlichkeit and Rückmeldung: the etiquette

    Um zeitnahe Vorstellung wird gebeten. Four urgency levels, four different waits: routinemäßig (routine — weeks), zeitnah (prompt — days), eilig (urgent — now-ish), dringender Verdacht — bitte umgehend vorstellen (immediately). The passive wird gebeten / erbeten is the fixed referral passive — never "ich bitte dringend". Marking everything eilig destroys your credibility; the candidate who calibrates urgency correctly shows clinical judgment, not just language. Über eine kurze Rückmeldung wäre ich dankbar. The reply request closes the collegial loop — wäre ich dankbar (Konjunktiv II, polite) with four options from kurze Rückmeldung to ausführlichen Befundbericht. And the contact line: Bei Rückfragen stehe ich gerne zur Verfügung. The referral that ends here is complete: question asked, urgency set, reply invited, contact given.

    Salutation, closing, and dictation traps

    A full Überweisung opens with the collegial salutation — Sehr geehrte Frau Kollegin, sehr geehrter Herr Kollege, — and closes with Mit freundlichen kollegialen Grüßen plus your name and practice stamp. Between them, the ten frames do the work. Three dictation traps catch candidates: the dative after überweisen (Ich überweise Ihnen — Ihnen, never dich), the accusative chain meinen Patienten Herrn Müller (both the possessive and the name take accusative endings), and the purpose preposition zur Mitbeurteilung (zu + der = zur, feminine). A fourth trap is register: the referral is written, so wäre ich dankbar (Konjunktiv II) is correct where spoken German might use würde mich freuen. Dictate the letter above twice — once slowly for the grammar, once at ward speed for the flow — and the collegial register becomes automatic.

    Frequently asked questions

    What belongs in a German Überweisung?

    Four pillars: Anlass (who and why — Ich überweise Ihnen … zur …), Befunde (previous findings and therapy), Fragestellung (the clinical question), Dringlichkeit plus Rückmeldung (urgency and reply request).

    How do I phrase the clinical question (Fragestellung)?

    Precisely: Fragestellung: Ausschluss einer koronaren Herzkrankheit. Or Bitte um MRT-Befundung und Therapieempfehlung. A vague Bitte um Mitbeurteilung alone is the classic referral failure.

    What is the difference between Mitbeurteilung, Abklärung and Weiterbehandlung?

    Mitbeurteilung: co-assessment alongside your care. Abklärung: workup of the unclear. Weiterbehandlung: handing ongoing care over. Zweitmeinung: second opinion. The wrong noun misdirects the referral.

    How do I mark urgency on a referral?

    Four levels: Routinemäßige Vorstellung ausreichend (weeks), Um zeitnahe Vorstellung wird gebeten (days), Eilige Vorstellung erbeten (urgent), Dringender Verdacht — bitte umgehend vorstellen (immediately). Calibrate honestly.

    What is the correct salutation for a referral letter?

    The collegial Sie-form throughout: Ich überweise Ihnen meinen Patienten … — with Ihnen dative-formal and the accusative double marking meinen Patienten Herrn Müller.

    Should I ask for a reply?

    Yes — Über eine kurze Rückmeldung wäre ich dankbar (Konjunktiv II, polite), from kurze Rückmeldung up to ausführlichen Befundbericht. It closes the collegial loop.
