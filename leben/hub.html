Why these tools exist

Moving to Germany means facing the Sperrkonto before you even land, the Anmeldung within 14 days of arrival, health insurance from day one, and a rental market where newcomers routinely overpay. Germans face their own maze: the Mietpreisbremse hardly enforces itself, and missing a Kündigungsfrist by one day costs three months of rent.

Each tool is built from the actual rules: the 2026 statutory Sperrkonto amounts (€11,904 for students, €13,092 for the Chancenkarte track), the official Chancenkarte points grid, the Vergleichsmiete + 10% rent cap formula, and the §573c BGB notice deadline. Where figures vary — city rents, one-time costs — the tools say so openly and show their sources.

For every community

These tools are for everyone building a life in Germany, whatever passport they hold. Newcomer tools are in English and work for all nationalities; where the law itself treats groups differently (EU citizens don't need a Sperrkonto, for example), that's stated as plain fact. Nothing here targets, excludes, or stereotypes any community.

Frequently asked questions

Are these Germany tools really free?

Yes — every tool runs entirely in your browser. No account, no signup, no fees, no email gate.

Who are these tools for?

Everyone building a life in Germany: newcomers of every nationality as well as long-time residents. Newcomer tools are in English; resident tools are in German, some with English summaries.

Is this legal advice?

No. The tools give orientation based on publicly documented rules and official figures. For binding advice, contact a Mieterverein, an immigration lawyer, or the relevant authority.

Where do the figures come from?

Each tool documents its sources on the page: statutory amounts, official rules, and 2026 cost data with honest estimate framing where exact figures vary.

Do the tools work on phones?

Yes — all tools are built mobile-first and work in any modern browser.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything is computed locally in your browser; nothing you type ever leaves your device.
